## 2. Night, beds, shelter
- Hostiles spawn only at **block light 0** (sky light ≤7 at night), ≥24 blocks from any player, on solid top faces. Night = ticks 13000–23000; bed usable 12542–23459 (any time in thunder; from ~12010 in rain). Undead burn from sunrise — but NOT under spruce canopy; creepers/spiders never burn.
- `players_sleeping_percentage=1` on this server: one bot in a bed for 100 ticks skips the night for everyone and clears thunder. **Sleeper duty**: 2 base bots never leave the hut after tick 11500; at 12542 `bot.sleep(bed)`; on error "monsters nearby" (hostile within 8 horiz / 5 vert) the partner kills it / close door, retry every 5 s. Hut: 5×5×3 cobble, door, 2 torches inside, roof, torch ring 6 blocks outside.
- Bed = 3 wool **of the same colour** + 3 planks. Wool: shear sheep (1–3 wool each, regrows on grass; shears = 2 iron) or kill (1 wool); or 4 string → 1 white wool (12 string = 1 bed; spiders drop 0–2). Known herd ≈(-128,76,35). Village houses contain beds (§7). Using a bed sets respawn even when sleeping is refused (daytime/monsters).
- Every bot eventually owns a bed near its workplace and sleeps in it once (right-click is enough to set respawn; keep one free block beside the bed or the respawn fails). Keeps respawns away from the spawn pond, enables corpse-runs.
- Until night-skip is reliable, at tick ≥12000 every surface bot WITHOUT (armour ≥7 pts + shield + stone sword) goes to shelter: nearest lit hut, mine tunnel, or box-in (§6). Miners below ground just keep mining (no day/night there). Builders do indoor jobs (smelt, craft, sort, haul inside lit area) — never scaffold in the dark.
- Phantoms appear for a player with ≥72000 ticks (3 days) without sleep/death — night-skipping by one bot does NOT reset the others; rotate sleepers or accept (they only spawn above sky-exposed players at night, which we skip anyway).

## 3. Food economy
**Hunger maths (Java):** food 0–20 + hidden saturation. 4.0 exhaustion = −1 saturation (then −1 food). Costs: sprint 0.1/m, jump 0.05, **sprint-jump 0.2**, swim 0.01/m, attack 0.1, take damage 0.1, mine block 0.005, **walking 0**, **natural regen 6.0 per HP**. Regen only at food ≥18 (1 HP/4 s); at food 20 with saturation 1 HP/0.5 s. Sprint impossible at food ≤6. Starvation on Normal stops at 1 HP. Eating takes 32 ticks.
- ⇒ A sprinting bot burns a whole steak (8+12.8) every ~830 m ≈ 2.5 min; a walking bot burns ~0. Healing 20 HP costs 30 food points. **Set `movements.allowSprinting=false` for all routine travel/hauling** (walk 4.32 m/s vs sprint 5.61 m/s); sprint only to flee/fight or when FOOD chest >300.
- Eat rule: if hp<20 eat at food ≤17 (keep regen on); else eat when `20-food ≥ foodPoints(item)` (no waste). Never eat with a hostile <8 blocks. Priority: highest saturation first when hurt. Emergency only: raw meat, rotten flesh (80% Hunger effect).

| Food | pts | sat | Source effort for bots |
|---|---|---|---|
| cooked beef / porkchop | 8 | 12.8 | breeding pen (wheat / carrots) — best end state. Snowy taiga DOES spawn sheep 12/chicken 10/pig 10/cow 8 (weights) but mostly at chunk generation — they don't come back: **capture and breed, never kill the last 2** |
| cooked mutton / salmon | 6 | 9.6 | sheep pen (also wool) / fishing |
| cooked chicken | 6 | 7.2 | **chicken pen from seeds — cheapest loop, also feathers+eggs** |
| baked potato / bread / cooked cod | 5 | 6 | potato farm ≫ wheat (1 plant→~3 potatoes, no crafting, vs 3 wheat→1 bread) |
| carrot 3/3.6 · apple 4/2.4 · sweet berries 2/0.4 · golden carrot 6/14.4 (farmer villager) | | | berries = emergency only |
- **Fishing (the right call in this biome):** wait 5–30 s per bite (needs sky above bobber, else ×2), 85% fish (of which 60% cod, 25% salmon), 5% treasure (enchanted books, bows, rods, saddles!). ≈2 edible fish/min/bot ≈ 11 food pts/min. Rod = 3 sticks + 2 string. Ice hole refreezes unless a torch is adjacent. **3 permanent fishers feed 30 walking bots**; they cannot feed 30 sprinting ones.
- **Cooking:** furnace 10 s/item; **smoker 5 s/item** (furnace + 4 logs); **campfire: 4 items at once, 30 s each, NO fuel** (3 sticks + 1 coal + 3 logs) — right-click with raw food, pick up the popped item. Target: 2 smokers + 2 campfires by the FOOD chest.
- **Crop farm:** farmland hydrated within 4 blocks of water (same y) → 9×9 with centre water = 80 plants; needs light ≥9 above the crop (torches → grows at night). In snowy biomes sky-exposed water freezes when block-light <10 next to it → **cover the water block with a slab/trapdoor or put a torch adjacent**. Seeds: break short grass/ferns (12.5%). Growth: mean ≈24 min in alternating rows of different crops (80% done by 31 min), ≈40–50 min in a solid single-crop field / at edges; bone meal (1 bone → 3) adds 2–5 stages. Wheat first (needed for cows/sheep), **switch plots to potatoes/carrots the moment one is obtained** (zombie rare drop 0.83% each, village farms, shipwreck/outpost chests). Fence + torch the plot (trampling: never jump on farmland; harvest by walking, not jumping).
- **Breeding:** cow/sheep = wheat, pig = carrot/potato/beetroot, chicken = any seeds, rabbit = carrot/dandelion. 5 min parent cooldown, baby adult after 20 min. Pen 9×9 fenced, keep ≥6 adults per species, cull only adults above that. Lead animals by holding their food (they follow within ~6 blocks) — walk, don't sprint. Eggs thrown = 1/8 chick.
- Distribution: ONE FOOD chest (-42,87,16); every bot leaving base takes 8 cooked items (not more — lost on death); miners' depot chest gets 32.

## 5. Lighting / spawn-proofing
Torch = light 14, falls 1 per block (taxicab). ## 6. Combat doctrine
**Numbers:** attack cooldown ticks = 20/attackSpeed → sword 1.6 → 12.5, full damage from tick **12** (use 13 under lag); stone/wood axe 0.8 → 25; iron axe 0.9 → 23; diamond axe 1.0 → 20; pickaxe 1.2 → 17. Hitting earlier scales damage by 0.2+0.8·((t+0.5)/T)². Damage: sword wood 4 / stone 5 / iron 6 / diamond 7; axe wood 7 / stone 9 / iron 9 / diamond 9. Crit ×1.5: falling, not sprinting, not in water/on ladder, cooldown ≥84.8% → jump, attack on the way DOWN (dragon is crit-immune). Targets are hit-immune 10 ticks → never swing faster than 2/s. Reach 3.0. Mob HP: zombie/skeleton/creeper/blaze 20 (zombie 2 armour), spider 16, enderman 40, dragon 200. Normal damage: zombie 3, spider 2, skeleton arrow 2–5, creeper ≤43, enderman 7, blaze fireball 5+fire.
- `mineflayer-pvp` already times the 1.9 cooldown and drops/raises an off-hand shield around hits; it does NOT pick weapons, crit, or judge whether to fight. Always equip weapon first; stop auto-eat/dig during a fight; one owner of the pathfinder goal.
- **Engage test (run every 10 ticks, not 3 s):** fight iff weapon ≥ stone sword AND hp ≥ 12 AND food ≥ 7 AND (shield OR armour ≥ 7) AND hostiles within 10 ≤ 2 AND target ≠ creeper (unless shield/bow). Otherwise **evade**: walk away (all common mobs are slower than a walking player except spiders/baby zombies), break line of sight vs skeletons, go to the nearest lit shelter. If cornered/hp<8: **box-in** — dig 1 down where safe or stay, place blocks at head+sides (needs 6 cobble), eat, wait for hp ≥16 and threats gone.
- **Shield (1 iron + 6 planks, 100% frontal block incl. arrows and creeper blasts):** off-hand, `bot.activateItem(true)` to raise (effective after 5 ticks), `bot.deactivateItem()` to lower; face the threat. Loop vs melee: raised → lower → hit at full cooldown → raise. Vs skeleton: advance with shield up, lower only to hit. Axe hits disable it 5 s (vindicators, piglin brutes).
- **Creeper:** fuse starts ≤3 blocks, 30 ticks, cancels if target >7 blocks or out of sight. Without shield/bow: keep ≥6 blocks, never path past it; near builds lure it away (blast destroys blocks + all dropped items). With shield: face it, shield up = 0 damage. Melee method: sprint-hit (knockback) → back off 4 blocks → repeat at cooldown; stone axe crit 13.5 → 2 hits.
- **Skeleton** (accurate ≤15 blocks): fight only with shield, or under cover; otherwise break LOS — they burn by day. **Spider**: hostile only in dark, climbs walls → roof your box. **Enderman**: never look at head (pitch check), ignore; if aggro'd stand under a 2-high ceiling or in water. **Baby zombie**: fast, fits 1-high gaps → box-in needs full seal. **Drowned** near water with trident: avoid shores at night. **Stray** (this biome, slowness arrows): same as skeleton, more reason for shields.
- Team play: miners' guard stands at the tunnel mouth; never more than 3 bots melee one mob (they hit-block each other); loot goes to whoever is safest. Bow (3 sticks + 3 string; arrows = flint+stick+feather → 4): charge 20 ticks, use `minecrafthawkeye` for ballistics — guards and expedition only.

## 9. Death, banking, hazards (keepInventory OFF)
- Items lie 6000 ticks (5 min) **only while the chunk is loaded**; XP mostly lost. On `death` event: write `{bot,x,y,z,dim,t,inventory summary}` to `bots/deaths/<bot>.json`. Respawned bot: if death spot ≤150 blocks and cause ≠ lava/void → grab 4 food + stone sword from TOOLS chest → walk back → `pickupNear(radius 8)`; if night/hostiles there, the nearest armed teammate does it instead. Never chain-die: max 1 retry.
- **Kit whitelist when leaving base:** tools in use (+1 spare pick), weapon, shield, armour worn, 8 food, 16–32 torches, 32 cobble, 16 sticks, table, bucket if role needs. Everything else (ores, ingots, diamonds, surplus, spare tools) goes into chests at EVERY visit; miners bank at the hub chest every 15 min or on first diamond/8 raw iron. Diamonds/ingots never travel on a bot that is about to fight or explore.
- `rcon kill` unstuck: only after unwedge + `stairUp`/`climbOut` failed for >60 s AND (inventory is junk OR a teammate can reach the spot). Log it. Fix build_a watchdog and build_b `rconKill` comments/logic ("keep_inventory is on" is false).
- Drowning: breath lasts 15 s then 2 HP/s. If `bot.oxygenLevel < 12` → hold `jump` to surface, then level-pitch swim to `shoreNear`. Falls: damage = (blocks fallen − 3); `maxDropDown=3`; `sneak` while placing at height ≥4 or on any 1-wide edge; scaffolds 2 wide; tall builds get a water block or hay bales at the base of climb routes. Lava: never dig up; plug before passing; fire → water bucket or run to water. Powder snow (groves/snowy slopes; looks like snow block): sink + freeze (1 HP/2 s after 7 s) — already in `blocksToAvoid`; leather boots make it walkable. Berry bushes slow+damage — avoid in paths. Human PvP killed bots 20×: never retaliate, step away from humans' reach; commander may ask the owner about `pvp=false`.
- Spawn hardening (build_b job): cobble-cap the pond on the corridor, fence/fill the gash lip at x −10..−25, light ±16, signposted 3-wide road spawn→base→site.

## 13. mineflayer idioms worth standardising (from Voyager / Mindcraft / plugin sources)
- Reflex layer every 5–10 ticks beside the role loop (Mindcraft "modes"): water overhead→jump; falling block overhead→step aside; on fire→water; hp<6 or burst damage→flee; stuck 20 s within 2 blocks→move 5 away; item within 8→collect when idle; dark→torch (5 s cooldown).
- Pathfinder: try `canDig=false` first, then allow digging (Mindcraft `digCost 10, placeCost 2`); `path_update.status`: only `noPath` is final, `partial/timeout` = keep going; `exclusionAreasBreak/Place` (cost ≥100) to protect builds/farms/base; add deepslate/netherrack to `scafoldingBlocks`; keep `dontMineUnderFallingBlock`, `dontCreateFlow`.
- `mineflayer-pvp` respects cooldown (MaxDamageOffset ±2 ticks) and auto-handles an off-hand shield (lowers to hit, raises ~150 ms later, raises on creeper fuse) — so simply EQUIPPING a shield off-hand upgrades every existing fight. It never picks the weapon: equip first. It owns the pathfinder while active.
- Eating swaps the held item → mutex with dig/fight. Furnace: `openFurnace` → `putFuel`/`putInput`/`takeOutput` sequentially, take output BY HAND (XP). Sleep errors are informative ("monsters nearby" → kill/seal, retry). Buckets: look at block centre + `activateItem()`, then verify inventory (known flaky: mineflayer#3731). Bow: `activateItem()` → 20 ticks → `deactivateItem()`; `minecrafthawkeye` for aim. Breeding: equip food → `bot.useOn(entity)`/`activateEntity`. Trading: `openVillager` → `bot.trade(v,i,n)`.
- Voyager lessons: wrap primitives (`mineBlock/craftItem/smeltItem/killMob/exploreUntil`) and forbid raw calls in role code; verify every task by inventory delta (their critic is worth 73% of performance); fail counter → "explore elsewhere" instead of retrying the same block. JARVIS-1: re-plan on tool break. Odyssey: recursive prerequisites (`needIronPick → needIron → needStonePick …`).
